# For players

{% hint style="info" %}
MUSDash is live on testnet. You can play with MUSD, Y or P.
{% endhint %}

## 1. Get chips

You can play with MUSD, Y or P. Each position uses one kind.

* **To get Y and P:** zap BTC into a series, or deposit a veBTC lock. See [Doors](../how-it-works/doors.md).
* **To get MUSD:** borrow it on Mezo against your BTC, or swap for it on Mezo's DEX.

Y is the safest chip: it is only your future rewards, and your BTC is never at risk. P puts your BTC at stake. With Y or P you don't need MUSD: fees and losses are paid by handing the Bank that value of your chips.

While your Y is posted as chips, its rewards are credited to your position and paid to you when it closes.

## 2. Pick a side and a floor

* **Bull** if you think BTC goes up, **Bear** if down.
* **Low floors** (1.5x to 3x) need a big move to collapse. **High floors** (8x to 10x) pay more, but a move of about 8% to 10.5% against you collapses them.

Check the collapse price before you open. The app shows it for every position.

## 3. Watch, climb or close

* **Close** any time to take what your position is worth, minus a 0.1% fee.
* **Climb** to a higher floor to bet bigger with the same chips. Agaruzo!
* **Drop** to a lower floor to play safer.

## 4. Survive and collect

* **Survivor rewards:** when someone on your side collapses, you get a share of their penalty, more if your position is bigger and older.
* **Seat premiums:** on floors 7 to 10, each new seat after yours pays a premium that you share with the others on your floor.
* **Jackpot tickets:** every position earns tickets for the weekly jackpot: size x time held.

All of it is added when you close.

## 5. Optional extras

* **Loop:** if you came in with BTC or a veBTC lock, turn the Loop on and your winnings become more BTC in your series lock. See [Loop](../how-it-works/loop.md).
* **Let an agent play:** give your AI agent a session with rules you choose. See [Juu Agents](../agents/README.md).
* **Play from a Juu account:** bet with MUSD borrowed from your own Mezo loan, with a safety guard. See [Doors](../how-it-works/doors.md).

## Costs

* Trading fee: 0.1% of position size to open, close or move floors.
* Seat premium: on floors 7 to 10 while the Climb Premium is on.
* Agent fee: only if an agent plays for you.
* Funding: if your side is the crowded one, you pay a small hourly fee. If your side is the quiet one, you receive it.

Read [Risks](../reference/risks.md) before you play.
