# FAQ

### When do I get my BTC back?

After your series end date. From one hour after that Thursday, the BTC is withdrawn from Mezo and you redeem each P for 1 BTC. Until then your BTC stays in Juu, inside the series lock at Mezo.

### Why can't Juu's series last only 30 days like veBTC locks?

They can be shorter or longer. Juu keeps each series lock permanent (full strength) and switches it back to a normal 30-day lock in the last month, so a series can end on any Thursday Juu picks.

### What happens if nobody starts the unlock on time?

Juu's keeper bot does it in the first hours of the unlock week. If it were missed, anyone could still call it, but the lock would end on a later Thursday and P would be paid later.

### Can I use my veBTC lock if it voted this week?

Not this week. Mezo only lets a vote be cleared after a new week starts. Deposit it next week.

### Where do Y rewards come from?

From Mezo: the fees and bribes of the reward pool the series lock votes for, plus Mezo's weekly BTC rebase.

### Is the P return really fixed?

If you buy P and hold it to the end date, you know the price you paid and you get 1 BTC per P. The return depends only on the price you paid. You still carry the risks on the [Risks](risks.md) page.

### Does MUSDash buy BTC when I bet?

No. Positions are synthetic. Your result is worked out from Mezo's BTC price and paid in MUSD.

### Who wins when I lose?

Your loss goes to the Bank, which pays the winners. When both sides are playing, losers on one side pay winners on the other through the Bank.

### Can my AI agent play for me?

Yes, through Juu Agents. Give it its own wallet, grant it a session with your rules on the Agent Gateway, and connect it through the MCP server or the gateway directly. It never needs your key. See [Juu Agents](../agents/README.md).

### What happens to my Y rewards if I bet with Y?

They are credited to your position. They count in its value and are paid to you when it closes.

### Why did the Climb Premium stop?

The kill switch turns it off for good when new top-floor seats in an epoch fall below half of the epoch before.

### Is this on mainnet?

No. Juu runs on Mezo testnet only.
